Dr. Hadi Shirouyehzad

Associate Professor of Industrial Engineering

Islamic Azad University, Najafabad Branch · Isfahan, Iran

A faculty member in industrial engineering since 2001 and head of the department's graduate program, he has also held management roles in industry, in planning and technology, industrial engineering and productivity improvement. He advises companies on strategic planning, organizational structure, process management and productivity, and was Editor-in-Chief of the International Journal of Applied Research on Industrial Engineering (2014–2019).

Dr. Reza Dabestani

Assistant Professor, Center of Marketing and Supply Chain Management

Nyenrode Business University · The Netherlands

An Assistant Professor at Nyenrode Business University, he researches data-driven business models, technology innovation and operational excellence, and teaches information systems and emerging technologies. Before that he spent seven years in the pharmaceutical industry: as industrial engineering and productivity manager at Tolidaru Pharmaceutical and as senior project development and investment expert at Alborz Investment Group, where he led business excellence (EFQM) programmes and sat on the boards of two pharmaceutical companies.
