The craft

From first line to final polish.

  1. I

    Design

    Every motif is drawn by hand with compass and pen, proportioned to the old geometric canons.

  2. II

    Shape

    Metal is raised and chased, wood cut into slivers finer than a match, silk dyed with plants and roots.

  3. III

    Gild

    Leaves of 24-carat gold are laid on with a squirrel-hair brush and burnished with agate.

  4. IV

    Seal

    The master signs and numbers each piece, and it leaves with a certificate of its making.
